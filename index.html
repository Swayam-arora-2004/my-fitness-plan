6-Day Push / Pull / Legs Split
Weekly Exercise Plan
Push/Pull/Legs × 2 — the best split for aesthetics. You hit each muscle group twice per week. Mon/Thu = Push (chest, shoulders, triceps), Tue/Fri = Pull (back, biceps), Wed/Sat = Legs + Core. Sets × Reps listed — choose your own weight. Focus on progressive overload: try to add a little weight or one extra rep each week.

Must Take
Vitamin D3 + K2
2,000 IU D3 + 100 mcg K2 · With breakfast (fat meal) · Daily
Your diet provides only ~90 IU/day from food — target is 600 IU minimum. 15 min sunlight helps but is unreliable. Deficiency causes fatigue, poor gym performance, low immunity, and low testosterone. K2 ensures calcium goes to bones, not arteries. ~₹250–300/month.
Food provides: ~15% of requirement. Gap: 85%. Cannot be fixed by diet alone.
CRITICAL
Vitamin B12
500 µg (methylcobalamin form) · Morning · With water · Daily
Your diet gives ~2.7 µg — just at the RDA. But vegetarian B12 from dairy has lower bioavailability. For gym performance, energy, nerve health, and red blood cell production, supplementing 500 µg ensures you never run deficient. Especially important since you're in a calorie deficit. ~₹150–200/month.
Food provides: ~2.7 µg (milk, curd, paneer, eggs) but low bioavailability. Supplement is insurance.
CRITICAL
Needed — Highly Recommended
Creatine Monohydrate
5g · Post-Workout · With water · Daily (even rest days)
The single most research-backed supplement for strength and muscle development. Vegetarians have ZERO dietary creatine (it's only in meat), so your baseline is empty — you'll see bigger gains than meat-eaters when you start. Helps build strength and muscle while in a calorie deficit. Take consistently — no loading needed. ~₹400–600 for 3 months (generic/bulk). Best ROI of any supplement.
Food provides: 0 mg (creatine only in meat). Complete gap.
NEEDED
Zinc
15–25 mg elemental zinc · With dinner · Daily
Your food covers ~8 mg but target is 8–10 mg minimum. Vegetarian zinc has poor bioavailability (phytates in grains block absorption). Zinc is critical for testosterone production, immune function, and recovery. Low zinc = slow gains, low energy, poor immunity. ~₹150/month.
Food provides: ~8 mg but only ~30% absorbed from plant sources. Effective intake ~2.4 mg.

Food provides: ~453 mg total magnesium. Supplement adds sleep-specific benefits beyond food quantity.
